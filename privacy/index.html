1. Who we are

Simvela is a mobile app that sells travel eSIMs. This document explains what data we collect, why, and who we share it with.

Data controller: Azer Abishov (registered sole proprietor, Azerbaijan) Contact: abishovazar@gmail.com

2. What we collect

Data Source Purpose
Email, name, account identifier Google or Apple Sign In Create your account, link your orders, provide support
Language preference App settings Show the interface and notifications in your language
Order data (package, country, price, date, status) At purchase Fulfil the order, show your history, accounting
eSIM data (ICCID, activation code, QR, data usage, validity) When an eSIM is issued and used Deliver the eSIM, show remaining data, send alerts
Device push token and platform When you allow notifications Send low-data, depleted and eSIM-ready alerts
Support chat messages and screenshots you send When you send them Answer your question

We do not collect: your location (GPS), your contacts, your photo library (only the image you explicitly pick), or advertising identifiers. The app contains no third-party advertising or analytics trackers.

Card details: Your card number, CVV and expiry never reach our servers. Payment happens on the licensed payment provider's own page. When it completes, the provider returns three things which we store alongside your order: whether the payment succeeded, the masked card number (e.g. 4*4033) and the *bank reference number (RRN). We keep these to identify the payment in a dispute or refund; they cannot be used to charge your card.

3. Where data is stored

Data is stored on Supabase Inc. infrastructure in the Japan (Tokyo) region. This means your data is transferred outside Azerbaijan and processed there. By using the app you consent to this transfer.

4. Who we share with

Party What they receive Why
Supabase Inc. All data above Database and server infrastructure (Tokyo)
eSIMAccess Only order id, package code and price To provision the eSIM. Your name and email are NOT sent
Anthropic PBC Support chat messages and images you send To generate the AI support reply. Your account id is not sent
Apple / Google Device push token and notification text To deliver the notification to your device
Payment provider Payment amount and order reference To take the payment

We do not sell your data and do not share it for advertising. We may disclose data to authorities where legally required.

5. Retention

  • Account data — while your account is active
  • Order and eSIM history — 5 years, for tax and accounting obligations
  • Support chat — for the current session; not retained long-term on our servers
  • Push token — deleted immediately when you disable notifications or sign out

6. Your rights

  • Access: ask what data we hold about you
  • Correction: ask us to correct inaccurate data
  • Deletion: delete your account inside the app — Profile → Delete account. This removes your profile, orders and eSIM records. ⚠️ An already-activated eSIM is not revoked and keeps working. ⚠️ Payment records may be retained where tax law requires it.
  • Object: turn notifications off at any time in Settings

To exercise these rights: abishovazar@gmail.com

7. Children

The app is not intended for anyone under 18. If we learn a user is under 18, we will delete their data.

8. Security

All traffic uses encrypted channels (HTTPS). The database is protected with row-level security — each user can only read their own records. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. If a change is significant we will notify you in the app. The effective date above indicates the current version.
