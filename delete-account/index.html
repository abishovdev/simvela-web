Simvela — Hesabın silinməsi / Account deletion

Simvela (developer: Azar Abishov, package az.simvela.app) lets you delete your account and everything linked to it at any time.

How to delete your account

In the app

  1. Open the Simvela app and sign in
  2. Go to Profile
  3. Tap Delete account
  4. Confirm

Deletion happens immediately and cannot be undone.

If you cannot open the app

Email abishovazar@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject Delete my account. We reply and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What is deleted

Everything we hold about you is removed from our database:

  • Your name and email address
  • Your Simvela account and sign-in identifier
  • Your order history
  • Your eSIM records
  • Push notification token
  • Support chat messages and any images you sent to support

What is kept

Nothing that identifies you is kept after deletion.

Aggregate, anonymous statistics that contain no information identifying you are not affected.

One thing to know before you delete

An eSIM that is already installed on your phone keeps working until its data or validity runs out. Deleting your Simvela account removes your records with us — it does not switch off a plan you are already using. But once the account is gone we can no longer show you the remaining data, send you expiry warnings, or look the eSIM up for support.

Deleting data without deleting your account

If you want specific data removed but want to keep your account, write to abishovazar@gmail.com and tell us what you want removed. We answer within 30 days.
